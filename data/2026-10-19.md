This gives rise to four different types of vortex interaction, where one set of length scales determines vortex interactions inside the bags and the other set between the bags. Hence, within the bag the vortices can repel or attract and the bags themselves can repel or attract depending on the hierarchy of four length scales. When vortices repel within the bag, solutions act similarly to those in type 1.5 multicomponent superconductors, but in a single component system.
