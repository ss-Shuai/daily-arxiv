Method: Decomposing the QGT into three-center bond indices weighted by oriented area, resolving contributions by orbital pairs to identify bonding character (ionic, covalent, hypervalent, metavalent).

Abstract: Electronic properties of materials depend both on band energies and quantum geometry of electronic states. Resolving these contributions by orbital pair reveals the donor--acceptor character that distinguish ionic, covalent, hypervalent, and metavalent bonding. We identify the bonding pattern for topology in materials: noncollinear three center bonds, which are the source of Berry curvature in solids. Bond fluctuation can be directly linked to electromagnetic response by resolving their energy spectrum, opening a quantitative route towards the design of physical properties of materials from bonding motifs.
